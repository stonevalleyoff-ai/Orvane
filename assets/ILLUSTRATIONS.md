# Illustrations Orvane

Trois illustrations d’univers créées avec l’outil de génération d’images intégré, puis exportées en WebP pour le site. Elles ne représentent pas des sorties de la simulation et ne décrivent aucune espèce réelle du catalogue.

Les versions `-900.webp` sont des variantes adaptées aux petits écrans. Tous les fichiers sont locaux ; aucun service d’images externe n’est requis.

## vallee.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final wide panoramic illustration for the website of Orvane, a serious ecosystem and evolution simulation shared by games. Create an exquisite, highly detailed natural history landscape painting, cinematic wide 3:2 composition. An untouched ancient living world: a winding jade river through lush temperate forest, distant layered mountains, a rocky escarpment, tiny subtly unfamiliar quadruped herbivores near the river, ferns and mossy stones foreground. Ecology and plausible biology, peaceful, mysterious, grounded speculative nature, no magic. Hand-painted editorial concept art with fine engraved botanical texture, sophisticated muted deep forest green and warm sage, soft pale golden morning mist and warm ivory sky. Rich middle ground panorama, foreground dark botanical silhouettes at edges, horizon in top third. No text, no lettering, no UI, no buildings, no people, no logo, no watermark. Premium museum atlas meets environmental concept art.

## littoral.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: environmental illustration for Orvane, a shared ecosystem simulation. Premium hand-painted natural-history expedition atlas painting of a wild coast where distinct habitats meet: dark coastal pine trees and ferns on foreground cliffs left, amber dry grassland and stone ridges in the far background, clear jade tidal lagoon with detailed underwater kelp and rocky reefs visible through water at bottom right, distant sea fading into pale ivory mist. Cohesive landscape, grounded sophisticated biological worldbuilding, no architecture, no people, no magical effects. Fine gouache and engraving textures, deep pine green, muted sage, warm parchment, mineral teal, subtle amber. Wide cinematic landscape 3:2, crisp foreground and layered atmospheric distance. No text, no labels, no borders, no logo, no watermark.

## specimen.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait editorial illustration for Orvane ecosystem simulation website. A beautifully observed natural history painting of a small plausible speculative forest animal, an elegant russet and olive six-legged browsing mammal with a long flexible tail, fine fur, two small antler-like cranial structures, seen in profile standing on a mossy fallen branch with tiny ferns and botanical stems. One central creature, believable anatomy, calm alert pose. Premium expedition atlas illustration in gouache, fine engraved details, muted sepia sage moss green and warm cream, restrained field-journal aesthetic. Pale warm ivory background with very subtle paper grain, generous empty space at upper edge, organic isolated composition without frame. Scientifically curious and sophisticated, no cute cartoon, no game UI, no magic, no text or labels, no letters, no numbers, no watermark. Vertical composition 2:3.
